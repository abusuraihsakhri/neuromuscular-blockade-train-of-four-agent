Train-of-Four Interpreter

Classify observed neuromuscular blockade using TOF count, post-tetanic count (PTC), and quantitative TOF ratio. The calculation runs locally in this browser without an account, server, or uploaded data.

Not for clinical decisions. This is an unvalidated educational interpretation aid. Do not use it to determine reversal-drug dose, administer treatment, or decide when to extubate. Obtain a validated quantitative measurement at the adductor pollicis and follow institutional protocols.

Clinical context

  • 2023 ASA guidelines emphasize quantitative monitoring at the adductor pollicis.
  • A measured TOF ratio ≥ 0.9 is the recommended recovery threshold before extubation; it does not replace clinical assessment.
  • TOF count 4/4 alone cannot establish adequate neuromuscular recovery.
  • No drug-selection or dose advice is provided here.

Source and limitations

Category boundaries follow the American Society of Anesthesiologists 2023 practice guidelines. Read the full guideline (DOI) or its PubMed record. This implementation has not undergone device, usability, or clinical validation.

Privacy: observations are evaluated in the browser. No analytics, storage, or third-party APIs. JSON export occurs only when requested. Do not enter identifying patient information.
